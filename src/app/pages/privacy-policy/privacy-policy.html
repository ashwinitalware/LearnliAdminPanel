1 Introduction

Welcome to Learnli ("we", "our", "us", or "the Platform"). Learnli is an interactive digital learning platform designed to provide comprehensive courses, quizzes, assignments, audio lectures, mentor guidance, and learning tracking for students and educators.

This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding the collection, storage, processing, and protection of personal data when you use the Learnli Mobile Application (available on Google Play) or our website (https://learnli.online).

Notice: By downloading, installing, or registering with the Learnli application, you acknowledge and agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

2 Information We Collect

We collect only the information necessary to fulfill our educational services and app security:

Category Data Types Collected Purpose
Personal Identification Full Name, Email Address, Mobile Number, Profile Picture, Date of Birth, Gender, Academic Grade/Class, State & City. Account creation, identity verification, student/instructor profile management.
Academic & Learning Data Enrolled courses, video/audio progress, quiz submissions, scores, assignment files, mentor communications, and completion certificates. Curriculum delivery, automated grading, learning analytics, and performance reports.
Referral & Loyalty Points Referral codes, invited user logs, earned reward points, and points redemption ledger. Operating and rewarding the Learnli referral system.
Financial & Billing Data Subscription tier, transaction reference IDs, invoice amounts. (Card & bank details are handled securely by PCI-compliant payment gateways like Razorpay/Stripe/Google Play and never saved on Learnli servers). Processing course fees, managing student/instructor subscriptions.
Device & Technical Diagnostics Device model, Android OS version, IP address, unique device IDs (Android ID, FCM Token), crash reports via Firebase Crashlytics. Troubleshooting issues, fraud prevention, push notifications, and performance optimization.

3 Device Permissions Requested

The Learnli Android application may request specific permissions to enable dynamic learning features:

  • Camera (CAMERA): Required when taking photos for profile avatars or capturing homework/assignment worksheets to submit for mentor review.
  • Storage & Media (READ_MEDIA_IMAGES / READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE): Used to upload notes, assignment documents, and pick profile pictures from device storage.
  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS): Used to send timely class reminders, quiz notifications, subscription renewal notices, and referral reward credits.
  • Microphone (RECORD_AUDIO): Used only if you participate in interactive audio lessons, voice search, or mentor voice sessions.
  • Internet & Network State (INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE): Required to fetch courses, stream audio/video content, and verify network connectivity.

4 How We Use Your Data

Your data is processed strictly for the following purposes:

  • Providing, maintaining, and improving the Learnli educational ecosystem.
  • Facilitating secure login authentication via Firebase and backend APIs.
  • Tracking academic progress, calculating quiz scores, and issuing course certificates.
  • Managing referral points, discounts, and subscription tiers.
  • Delivering push notifications regarding scheduled lessons and platform updates.
  • Ensuring platform integrity, security, and fraud protection.

5 Third-Party Services & SDK Disclosures

We work with trusted third-party SDKs to provide backend infrastructure, crash analytics, and notifications:

  • Google Play Services: Core Android framework services and in-app updates.
  • Google Firebase: Firebase Authentication, Cloud Messaging (FCM), Crashlytics, and Analytics.
  • Payment Gateways (Razorpay / Stripe / Google Play Billing): Secure handling of in-app subscriptions and course fee payments.

6 Data Sharing & Disclosure

Strict Commitment: We DO NOT sell, rent, or trade your personal or academic information to third-party advertisers or data brokers.

Data is shared only with:

  • Course Instructors & Mentors: Student name and enrollment status to evaluate coursework and answer academic questions.
  • Cloud & Infrastructure Vendors: Secure hosting and media delivery strictly bound by strict confidentiality terms.
  • Legal Authorities: When required by applicable law, court summons, or regulatory authorities.

7 Data Security

We prioritize data safety by implementing industry-standard security protocols:

  • All API communication is protected using TLS/HTTPS (1.3) encryption in transit.
  • Passwords, tokens, and sensitive fields are hashed and securely stored using modern cryptographic standards.
  • Role-based access controls and least-privilege principles are enforced for all internal databases.

8 Data Retention & Account Deletion (Google Play Compliance)

You maintain full control over your data. In full adherence to Google Play policies, users can request account and data deletion at any time:

How to Request Account Deletion
  1. In-App: Go to Profile > Settings > Account Security > Delete Account and confirm with your credentials.
  2. Web / Email: Send an email to privacy@learnli.online with the subject "Account Deletion Request" from your registered email address.

Timeline: Profile information, learning history, quiz scores, and credentials are permanently purged from active databases within 30 days. Financial records are retained only as required by statutory accounting laws.

9 Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Learnli serves students of various age groups. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13 without appropriate parental or guardian consent. We do not use student data for behavioral profiling or third-party advertising. Parents can contact us at privacy@learnli.online to review, update, or delete their child's information.

10 Your Rights & Choices

Under applicable privacy laws, you have the right to:

  • Access and receive a copy of your personal data.
  • Rectify inaccurate personal details in your profile settings.
  • Request the complete deletion of your account and data.
  • Withdraw consent for optional permissions (camera, notifications) at any time.
