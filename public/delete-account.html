Learnli - Account & Data Deletion

In accordance with Google Play's User Data policy and international data protection laws (including GDPR and Indian DPDP), Learnli provides all registered users (students and instructors) with straightforward mechanisms to permanently delete their account and personal data.

How to Request Account Deletion

Method 1: In-App Instant Account Deletion

  1. Open the Learnli Mobile Application on your device.
  2. Log in to your account.
  3. Tap on Profile (bottom-right tab) and select Settings.
  4. Scroll down to the Danger Zone / Account Security section.
  5. Tap on Delete My Account and confirm your password or OTP.
  6. Your account will be instantly deactivated and scheduled for permanent database removal.

Method 2: Web / Email Request

If you do not have access to the mobile application, you can submit a deletion request directly to our Privacy Team:

Send an email to privacy@learnli.online with:

  • Subject: "Learnli - Account Deletion Request"
  • Your registered full name, email address, and mobile number.
  • Brief reason (optional).
Types of Data Deleted vs. Retained:
  • Deleted Permanently: Profile information, phone number, login credentials, course progress, quiz test scores, certificates, and referral points balance.
  • Retained for Legal Compliance: Financial transaction records & invoices are retained only for the minimum period legally required for statutory tax and financial auditing purposes, after which they are safely purged.
