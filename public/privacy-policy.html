1 Introduction

Welcome to Learnli ("we", "our", "us", or "the Platform"). Learnli is an online educational application offering courses, live & recorded lectures, audio learning, quizzes, mentor guidance, and learning progress management for students and instructors.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, store, process, transfer, and protect your information when you access or use the Learnli Mobile Application (available on the Google Play Store and other distribution platforms) and our website at https://learnli.online.

Important: By downloading, registering with, or using Learnli, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with this policy, please do not access or use our application or services.

3 Device Permissions Requested

To provide enhanced learning features, the Learnli Android application may request the following device permissions. You can grant or revoke these permissions at any time via your device's Settings > Apps > Learnli > Permissions.

  • Camera Permission (android.permission.CAMERA): Used exclusively when you choose to take a photo for your profile avatar, scan study QR codes, or capture homework assignments and documents for evaluation.
  • Storage / Media Images Permission (READ_MEDIA_IMAGES / READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE): Allows you to upload study notes, assignment PDFs, and select images from your gallery for your student or instructor profile.
  • Notifications Permission (POST_NOTIFICATIONS): Used on Android 13+ to send class schedule reminders, quiz deadlines, mentor announcements, subscription renewal alerts, and referral reward notices.
  • Microphone / Audio Permission (RECORD_AUDIO): Used only when participating in interactive audio lessons, voice search, or mentor-led live doubt-clearing sessions.
  • Internet & Network State (INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE): Required to connect to our secure servers, stream video/audio lectures, verify logins, and check internet connectivity.

4 How We Use Your Information

We process your personal information for the following legitimate educational and business purposes:

  1. Delivering Educational Services: Creating student/instructor accounts, providing video and audio courses, delivering quizzes, issuing certificates, and tracking academic progress.
  2. Account Management & Authentication: Verifying credentials via secure token/OTP mechanisms (Firebase Auth or backend API) and maintaining account security.
  3. Processing Transactions: Facilitating course enrollments, validating active subscription tiers, and handling referral point balances.
  4. Communication & Support: Sending course reminders, schedule updates, security advisories, and responding to support tickets.
  5. Platform Safety & Quality: Detecting and preventing fraud, unauthorized access, abusive behavior, and enforcing our Terms of Service.
  6. App Optimization: Analyzing aggregate usage trends and crash logs to improve app responsiveness, UI layout, and server performance.

6 Data Sharing & Disclosure

Zero Sale of Data: We do NOT sell, rent, lease, or trade your personal information to third parties or advertising brokers for commercial purposes.

We only share data under the following limited circumstances:

  • With Instructors / Mentors: When you enroll in a course, the assigned instructor receives your student display name, course progress, and quiz scores for academic delivery and grading.
  • With Authorized Service Providers: As outlined in Section 5, strictly under confidentiality agreements.
  • For Legal & Regulatory Compliance: If required to do so by applicable law, court order, or governmental authority.
  • Business Transfers: In the event of a merger, acquisition, or asset transfer, your information may be transferred with prior notice to users.

7 Data Security

We employ industry-standard administrative, technical, and physical security measures to safeguard your personal data:

  • Data in Transit: All communications between the Learnli application and our servers are encrypted using modern Transport Layer Security (TLS/HTTPS 1.3).
  • Data at Rest: Sensitive credentials, tokens, and database records are encrypted with advanced encryption standards.
  • Access Control: Strict role-based access control (RBAC) ensures only authorized personnel can access infrastructure systems.
  • Security Audits: Periodic vulnerability reviews and patch updates to maintain platform resilience.

8 Data Retention & Account Deletion Policy

We retain your personal information for as long as your account remains active or as needed to provide you educational services. If you no longer wish to use Learnli, you have the right to request deletion of your account and associated data.

How to Delete Your Account & Associated Data

In full compliance with Google Play Developer Policies, you can delete your account using any of the following methods:

  • Directly from the Learnli Mobile App: Navigate to Profile > Account Settings > Danger Zone > Delete Account. Confirm your password/OTP to instantly initiate account deletion.
  • Via Web Request / Email: Send an email from your registered email address to privacy@learnli.online or support@learnli.online with the subject line "Account Deletion Request - [Your Registered Email/Phone]".

What Happens Upon Deletion: Your personal profile, login credentials, course progress, quiz submissions, and referral points are permanently removed from our active database within 30 days. Transaction and billing records will only be retained for the minimum statutory period required by taxation and accounting laws.

9 Children's Privacy (COPPA & Minor Protection)

Learnli provides educational content suitable for students, including minors. We are committed to protecting the privacy of young learners:

  • We do not collect unnecessary personal data from children under the age of 13 (or applicable local age of digital consent) without verified parental/guardian consent.
  • We do not engage in behavioral advertising, third-party user profiling, or commercial exploitation of student data.
  • Parents and legal guardians may review, request modifications to, or demand the deletion of their child's educational data at any time by contacting privacy@learnli.online.

10 Your Rights and Choices

Depending on your jurisdiction (including GDPR, CCPA, and Indian Digital Personal Data Protection Act), you enjoy the following rights:

  • Right to Access: Request a copy of the personal information we hold about you.
  • Right to Rectification: Edit or update inaccurate or incomplete information directly via your in-app profile.
  • Right to Erasure (Right to be Forgotten): Request the permanent deletion of your account and personal records.
  • Right to Restrict or Object: Restrict or object to certain non-essential processing activities.
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: Withdraw previously granted permissions (camera, notifications) anytime via device settings.
